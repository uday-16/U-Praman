How PRAMAN works

A transparent, 4-step decision support workflow designed for government & enterprise procurement specifications.

01

Add procurement requirement

Upload a tender document in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format, or enter specifications manually into the structured requirement form.

02

Requirement extraction & human review

PRAMAN extracts product categories, application domains, technical parameters, quantities, and quality requirements. Users can review, edit, confirm, or add missing items before matching.

03

Match & rank standards

Relevant Indian Standards are matched and ranked based on requirement coverage, technical relevance, active version currency, and Quality Control Order (QCO) statutory compliance.

04

Review evidence & generate report

Inspect why each standard matched using the "Why this standard?" drawer, review evidence clause excerpts, check related standards, and print/export a 10-section Procurement Standards Report.
