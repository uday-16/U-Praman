PRAMAN Demo Standard 001

Specification for Industrial Safety Helmets & Head Protection

Compare Add to report

Scope & Purpose

This specification prescribes physical and performance requirements, test protocols, and chin strap retention guidelines for industrial safety helmets used in construction sites, heavy industrial manufacturing, and civil engineering works.

Technical & Safety Requirements

1. Shock Absorption & Impact Energy (Clause 5.1)

Transmitted force to striker must not exceed 5.0 kN when subjected to 50J drop impact energy test.

2. Electrical Insulation Rating (Clause 6.3)

Leakage current shall not exceed 1.2mA under 1,200V AC testing for Class E rated headwear.

Supporting Evidence Clauses

Clause 5.1 — Impact Attenuation Evidence

"Each protective helmet helmet assembly shall absorb shock energy equivalent to a 5kg mass dropped from a height of 1 meter without structural cracking or headform contact."
