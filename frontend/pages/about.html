About PRAMAN

PRAMAN is a smart platform created to simplify the process of identifying and understanding relevant Indian Standards for procurement.

Finding the right standards for a product or technical requirement can often be difficult because a single requirement may involve multiple standards related to product specifications, testing, safety, quality, and certification. PRAMAN brings these requirements together to provide users with a clearer view of the standards that may apply to their procurement needs.

With PRAMAN, you can:

Find Relevant Standards

Identify Indian Standards related to a product or requirement.

Understand Requirements

Get a clearer understanding of the technical and quality requirements associated with a standard.

Discover Related Standards

Identify additional standards related to testing, safety, materials, installation, and other requirements.

Identify Missing Requirements

Highlight areas where important specifications or testing requirements may need further attention.

Stay Aware of Updates

Consider revisions, amendments, and replacement of older standards when reviewing requirements.

Support Better Procurement

Help users make informed decisions while preparing or reviewing procurement specifications.

PRAMAN aims to bridge the gap between complex standards and practical procurement needs, making standards easier to find, understand, and use.
