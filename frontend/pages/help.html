BIS Standards Matching & Recommendation Engine

How does the PRAMAN AI engine match tender requirements with Indian Standards?
PRAMAN parses uploaded procurement documents or entered specifications against the official Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) digital catalogue. It evaluates product classification, chemical composition tolerances, mechanical strength parameters, safety standards, and environmental application ratings. Scores are weighted by clause alignment and statutory QCO coverage.
What does the match score percentage (e.g. 96%) indicate?
The match score represents the percentage of technical parameters, material grades, and test methods in your requirement that are explicitly covered by the published standard. Scores above 85% are classified as "Strong Match", 70-84% as "Related Standard", and below 70% flag a potential technical review recommendation.
How are amendments and reaffirmed editions handled?
PRAMAN maintains real-time synchronization with Gazette of India notifications and BIS reaffirmation registers. Every recommended standard includes its latest revision year, reaffirmation date, and active amendment numbers (e.g. IS 2062:2025 with Amendment No. 2).

Quality Control Orders (QCO) & Mandatory Certification

How does PRAMAN verify Quality Control Order (QCO) compliance?
When a product falls under a mandatory QCO issued by DPIIT, Ministry of Steel, or Ministry of Electronics, PRAMAN flags the standard with a mandatory badge ("Mandatory QCO — ISI Mark Compulsory"). Procurement officers are alerted that non-certified bidding will violate General Financial Rules (GFR Rule 144).
Can I override a mandatory standard in a public tender?
Public procurement rules strictly prohibit procuring non-ISI marked items for goods covered under active QCOs unless explicit exemption has been granted by the relevant ministry. PRAMAN's audit trail records all recommendations and warnings for transparency.

Document Upload & Requirement Parsing

Which file formats are supported for automatic tender parsing?
PRAMAN supports PDF tender documents, Microsoft Word files (.docx), and plain text files (.txt). Scanned PDFs are processed via optical character recognition to extract technical specification schedules and Bill of Quantity (BOQ) line items.
How do I export the compliance evidence report?
From the Results or Report view, click "Export Official PDF" or "Export CSV Matrix". The system generates a formatted compliance record complete with BIS clause references, requirement mapping matrices, and officer sign-off metadata for GeM bid filing.
