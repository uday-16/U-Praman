PROCUREMENT STANDARDS GUIDANCE

Indian Standards Procurement Compliance

Framework and best practices for incorporating Indian Standards (IS), Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and test certifications into procurement tender documents.

📜

Mandatory Standard Inclusion

Under General Financial Rules (GFR) and Ministry directives, procurement specifications must reference active Indian Standards wherever published by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

🛡️

Quality Control Orders (QCO)

Items notified under Quality Control Orders mandate ISI certification mark. Procurement specifications cannot accept uncertified alternatives without explicit statutory exemptions.

🔍

Version Currency Verification

Always reference standard codes with their latest revision year or state "latest revision" to prevent vendors from supplying under superseded technical specifications.

Recommended Tender Clause Formatting

Standard Clause Template A: General Equipment
"The supplied material/equipment shall conform in all respects to PRAMAN Demo Standard 001 (Specification for Industrial Safety & Protective Equipment) with all up-to-date amendments. Valid BIS license or NABL test certificate must accompany delivery."
Standard Clause Template B: Civil & Infrastructure
"Steel reinforcement bars shall strictly comply with PRAMAN Demo Standard 002 (High Strength Deformed Steel Bars for Concrete Reinforcement). Manufacturer test certificates (MTC) conforming to Clause 8 shall be mandatory for batch acceptance."
